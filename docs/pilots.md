# Two-gym pilot plan

## Design

Launch at two **local** gyms with different enough conditions to learn something useful (for example, one with showers and one without), but record those differences rather than claiming a controlled experiment. Start with a common core assortment. Record launch dates and run at least eight weeks of comparable observations per site, excluding outages from available-for-sale time but reporting them explicitly.

## Before launch

- Signed placement terms, approved SKU/price list, insurance/permissions, installation and removal plan.
- Tested vend and refund for each slot, payment reconciliation, baseline stock count, and support contact.
- Site record, machine record, SKU catalog, service schedule, incident log, and weekly report prepared.

## Weekly scorecard

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Units and sales by SKU/site | Paid vends and net collected revenue after refunds, excluding tax |
| Availability | Hours each slot is stocked and functioning / scheduled open hours |
| Vend success | Successful deliveries / attempted paid vends |
| Sell-through | Units sold / units stocked during the period; interpret alongside availability |
| Contribution | Formula in `economics.md`, including service labor and travel |
| Service effort | Minutes on-site, travel, restock and support by site |
| Complaints | Count, issue type, refund time and resolution |
| Member and manager feedback | Short voluntary qualitative notes, no identifying customer details |

## Decision gates

Set numerical targets **before launch** after obtaining machine quotes and site terms: acceptable vend success, availability, contribution, payback horizon, service time, and maximum unresolved complaints. Record targets in each site record. At weeks 2 and 4 adjust only with a logged reason; at week 8 decide per site: continue, change assortment/price/service, relocate, or stop. Do not call Phase 1 complete solely because two machines are installed: both must run, be serviced and measured long enough to reveal the operating process. If targets are missed, document why and repeat the relevant test.

## Phase 1 exit package

Two signed site agreements, machine and SKU comparison, eight weekly scorecards per site, ledger/reconciliation, support and service history, true economics, decision log, exceptions, revised SOPs, and an explicit go/no-go for automation. Remove or anonymize commercial and personal data before sharing publicly.
