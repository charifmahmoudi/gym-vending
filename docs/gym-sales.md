# Finding and pitching gyms

## Prospecting sequence

1. Build a local list of gyms within service radius. Prioritize locker-room access, showers, staffed hours, stable member traffic, and a decision-maker willing to run an eight-week trial.
2. Record existing amenities and likely placement. Do not assume that a women’s restroom allows commercial fixtures or that every member can access it.
3. Ask for a short discovery call and then a site walk. Record objections and requested changes, including free menstrual supplies, competitor exclusivity, accessibility, insurance, and custodial needs.
4. Send the site-specific proposal below and negotiate a written pilot agreement.

## Short outreach draft

> Subject: Small essentials vending pilot for [Gym]
>
> Hi [Name], I’m piloting a compact essentials machine for members who forget deodorant, hair ties, or shower products. I’d like to see whether it would be useful at [Gym]. I handle stocking, payment support, cleaning of the unit, and member refunds. Could we do a 15-minute walkthrough to review your existing amenities and a suitable location? I can then send a site-specific trial proposal. Thanks, [Name]

## Proposal checklist

Include photos/dimensions and floor plan; proposed SKU list and prices; what remains free; trial start/end and review dates; installation/removal costs and responsibility; electrical/network needs; restocking frequency and access; commission or rent and its calculation; payment/tax/insurance responsibilities; cleaning and incident response; sales reporting; customer support/refunds; signage and privacy; termination, relocation and damage terms. State any exclusivity explicitly. The gym must approve the final assortment and placement.

## Agreement gate

Record authorized signer, executed agreement reference, approved placement, start/end dates, access method, emergency contact, permission for photos/signage, insurance requirements, revenue-share formula, payment schedule, ownership and removal obligations. Have a qualified local adviser review terms if needed; this playbook is not a legal template.
