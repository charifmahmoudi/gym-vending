# Selecting a vending machine

## Requirements to verify with the actual site

| Dimension | Questions and acceptance check |
| --- | --- |
| Fit | Measure machine, door swing, delivery path, service clearance, and accessible circulation; gym approves a marked floor plan. |
| Dispensing | Test the smallest pad/wipe packet and largest bottle or pouch in the intended slots; verify drop protection and jam recovery. |
| Payments | Tap/card support, fees, refunds, reconciliation exports, offline behavior, PCI responsibilities, and who owns the merchant account. |
| Telemetry | Per-slot sales, stock and fault exports, timestamps, API availability, and data ownership. Manual export is acceptable in Phase 1. |
| Power/network | Outlet and load, Wi-Fi/cellular coverage, connectivity cost, and behavior during outages. |
| Safety/service | Stable mounting, tamper resistance, cleaning access, electrical certification, warranty, parts availability, local repair and response time. |
| Cost | Purchase or lease, freight, installation, tax, monthly software, processing, connectivity, repairs, removal, financing and residual value. |

Request comparable quotes from at least three suppliers or machine options. For each, record vendor, model, new/used condition, capacity and slot adjustability, actual SKU test results, lead time, references, full 24-month cost, and contract cancellation terms. Score fit, vend reliability, payments/export, support, and total cost 1–5; explain weights and disqualify any option that fails site approval or actual vend tests. A quoted capacity is not proof that your products fit.

**Purchase gate:** two signed or otherwise firm site commitments compatible with the model, a tested assortment, a service plan, and a financial scenario that can tolerate slower sales and downtime. Leasing or a smaller used unit may reduce pilot capital at risk; compare the complete costs rather than choosing on sticker price.
