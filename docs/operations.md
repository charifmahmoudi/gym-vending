# Manual operating workflow

## 1. Define the pilot market

Choose a service radius that one operator can cover reliably. Record the radius, travel time, target gym types, expected foot traffic, showers/pool availability, hours, existing amenities, and a contact for each prospect. Do not treat a guess about female membership or restroom visits as a measured count.

## 2. Qualify sites

Inspect the proposed location with the gym manager. Count accessible outlets, measure footprint and doorway, verify connectivity and signal, observe accessibility and cleaning constraints, check sightline without compromising privacy, and document who can enter to service it. Ask what the gym already gives away and whether its lease or policy permits a vending machine and a revenue share. Use `templates/site.md`.

## 3. Compare machines and commercial terms

Run `docs/machine-selection.md`. Get written quotes including delivery, installation, payment processing, telemetry, software, warranty, parts, and removal. Do not order until site approvals, fit, and a viable financial case are documented.

## 4. Pitch and agree

Use `docs/gym-sales.md`. Send a one-page proposal specific to the site. Capture all revisions; have the gym approve placement, power, access, cleaning, liability, signage, payment, reporting, revenue share, and termination in writing. Obtain insurance and any required permits or approvals before installation. Requirements vary by location; verify locally.

## 5. Source and configure

Select sealed retail-ready SKUs from `docs/products-inventory.md`; inspect expiration, temperature needs, packaging integrity, dimensions, and machine compatibility. Keep invoices, lot/expiry where relevant, and an initial stock count. Set prices from landed cost, fees, taxes, commission, and target margin; run a test vend for every slot.

## 6. Launch

Photograph approved placement and stocked slots, verify payment/refunds, display a support contact, establish an outage and refund procedure, and record starting cashless totals and inventory. Obtain gym sign-off. Record launch date separately for each pilot.

## 7. Service and support

At a scheduled cadence, reconcile telemetry to physical stock, refill, rotate older inventory first, inspect seals/expiry, clean approved surfaces, test payment and vend, and log downtime and time spent. For complaints: acknowledge, identify machine/time/slot, refund promptly when appropriate, isolate unsafe or defective stock, and record root cause and resolution. Never use restroom cameras or collect identifiable customer data to infer demand.

## 8. Weekly review

For each site and SKU calculate units sold, gross sales, net revenue, landed product cost, fees, commission, shrink, refunds, service minutes, stockout hours, and contribution. Record decisions to retain, replace, reprice, or relocate and the evidence behind them. Follow `docs/pilots.md` for thresholds.

## Decision log convention

Every decision gets: date, site, owner, proposed action, input data and source, assumptions, alternatives, approval, action taken, expected result, observed result, and exception or follow-up. This is the training and evaluation trail for Phase 2; it is not permission to train a model on private gym or customer data.
